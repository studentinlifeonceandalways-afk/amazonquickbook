Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 15, 2026

📋 Overview

Amazon Quick Book ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Chrome extension. We do NOT collect any personal identifiable information (PII) about you.

1. Information We Collect

We DO NOT collect any personal information such as:

  • ❌ Your name, email address, or contact details
  • ❌ Your IP address or location data
  • ❌ Your browsing history outside Amazon
  • ❌ Your payment or financial information
  • ❌ Any personally identifiable information (PII)

What We DO Collect (Anonymous Product Data):

  • ✅ ASIN - Amazon product identifier (public)
  • ✅ Product Title - Public product name
  • ✅ Product Price - Current product price
  • ✅ Price Currency - Currency symbol ($, £, €, etc.)
  • ✅ Product URL - Amazon product page link
  • ✅ Domain - Which Amazon marketplace (amazon.com, amazon.in, etc.)
  • ✅ Bookmark Count - How many times a product was bookmarked
  • ✅ Image URL - Product image link (public)
  • ✅ Timestamps - First and last bookmark dates

Important: All data collected is completely anonymous and cannot be traced back to any individual user. We do not store any user identifiers, cookies, or tracking tokens.

2. How We Collect Information

Information is collected only when you voluntarily bookmark a product by clicking the "☆ Bookmark" button on Amazon. The extension extracts public product information available on the Amazon product page.

  • Local Storage: Your bookmarks are stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API.
  • Supabase Database: Anonymous product statistics are sent to our secure database to track popular products (no user data).
  • No Tracking: We do not use cookies, web beacons, or any tracking technologies.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Display Bookmarks: To show your saved products in the extension sidebar.
  • Price Tracking: To convert prices to different currencies for comparison.
  • Affiliate Links: To append your Amazon Associate tags to product links (if enabled).
  • Anonymous Analytics: To understand which products are most bookmarked (aggregated, no user data).
  • Extension Improvement: To improve product data extraction accuracy.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase - Secure database hosting for anonymous product statistics. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Amazon - Product data is sourced from Amazon websites. Amazon Privacy Policy
  • Chrome Storage API - Local storage of your bookmarks. Google Privacy Policy

These services may have their own privacy policies. We do not share any personal data with them as we don't collect any.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Local Storage: Your bookmarks are stored locally on your device. You can clear them anytime using the "Clear All" button.
  • Supabase Database: Anonymous product data is stored securely with encryption at rest.
  • No Cloud Sync: Your bookmarks are not synced across devices unless you manually export them.
  • Data Retention: Anonymous product data is kept indefinitely for analytics. You cannot delete it as it's not linked to you.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • Delete Bookmarks: Remove individual bookmarks or clear all from the sidebar.
  • Disable Affiliate Links: Toggle affiliate links on/off in settings.
  • Opt-out of Analytics: Since we collect no user data, there's nothing to opt out of.
  • Uninstall Extension: Remove the extension completely from Chrome to delete all local data.

7. Affiliate Links Disclosure

Amazon Quick Book may add affiliate tags (Amazon Associate tags) to product links when enabled. This means we may earn a commission if you make a purchase through these links. This does not affect your price and helps support the development of this extension.

You can disable affiliate links at any time in the extension settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Our extension is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Since we collect no personal data, this is not applicable.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance.

GDPR (Europe): Since we collect no personal data, GDPR does not apply. We have no user data to export or delete.
