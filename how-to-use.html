🚀 Getting Started
1

Install the Extension

Add Amazon Quick Book from the Chrome Web Store. After installation, you'll see the extension icon 📦 in your browser toolbar.

2

Visit Any Amazon Site

Go to any Amazon marketplace - amazon.com, amazon.co.uk, amazon.de, or any of the 22+ supported domains.

3

Start Bookmarking

Look for the green ☆ Bookmark button on product pages or search results. Click it to save products to your collection.

💡 Pro tip: Click the extension icon in your toolbar to open the sidebar, or it will open automatically when you visit Amazon pages.
⭐ How to Bookmark Products

📄 Product Pages

On any product detail page, you'll find a prominent green button below the price. Click once to bookmark, click again to remove.

🔍 Search Results

Each product in search results gets its own compact bookmark button. Perfect for saving multiple products while browsing.

♾️ Infinite Scroll

New products loading as you scroll? No problem! The extension automatically detects and adds buttons to new items.

✨ What gets saved? The extension captures product title, price (in local currency), ASIN, rating, review count, product image, and the URL.
📋 Sidebar - Your Bookmark Dashboard
📊

Stats at a Glance

Top of the sidebar shows your total bookmarks count and the combined value of all saved products (converted to your preferred currency).

🔍

Search Your Bookmarks

Use the search bar to filter bookmarks by product title or ASIN. Results update instantly as you type.

📤

Share Products

Each bookmark has a 📤 Share button. Share via WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, Twitter, or copy the link to your clipboard.

🗑️

Manage Bookmarks

Remove individual bookmarks with the ✕ Remove button, or use Clear All to wipe your entire collection.

💱 Multi-Currency Support

Amazon Quick Book supports 22 different Amazon marketplaces, each with its own currency. Here's how currency conversion works:

💰 Local Price Display

Each product shows its original price in the marketplace's local currency (USD, GBP, EUR, INR, JPY, etc.).

💵 USD Conversion

All prices are automatically converted to USD using exchange rates, giving you a unified view of your total spending.

🌍 Currency Selector

Use the dropdown in the sidebar to view your total in any of 16 currencies - perfect for international shoppers!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my bookmarks stored?

All bookmarks are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API. They sync across devices if you're signed into Chrome with sync enabled.

Does this work on Amazon mobile site?

Yes! The extension works on both desktop and mobile versions of Amazon. The bookmark button adapts to the mobile layout.

How do I update exchange rates?

Click the 💱 button in the sidebar to open the Exchange Rates page. You can manually edit rates or click "Fetch Latest Rates" for automatic updates.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Clearing browser data may remove your bookmarks. We recommend exporting important bookmarks or ensuring Chrome sync is enabled.

Is there a limit to how many bookmarks I can save?

No, you can save as many products as you want! The only limit is Chrome's storage quota (around 10MB for local storage).
