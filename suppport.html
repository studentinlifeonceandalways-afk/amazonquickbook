❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find my extension version? ▼
Go to chrome://extensions, find "Amazon Quick Book", and look for the version number next to the extension name.
Why aren't affiliate links working? ▼
Make sure you've configured your Amazon Associate tags correctly in the extension settings. You need to replace the placeholder 'yourtag-20' with your actual affiliate tag.
How do I clear all bookmarks? ▼
Open the sidebar by clicking the extension icon, then click the "Clear All" button at the top. This action cannot be undone.
Does this work on all Amazon sites? ▼
Yes! The extension supports all 22 Amazon domains including .com, .co.uk, .de, .in, .ca, .com.au, .jp, and many more.
Is my data stored securely? ▼
All bookmarks are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's secure storage API. No personal data is sent to external servers except anonymous product stats for popularity tracking.
